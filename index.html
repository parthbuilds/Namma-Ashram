EXPLORE ALL SPACES & VENUES

A Sanctuary for
Mindful Living

01. Wholesome Café

Following Macrobiotic & Ayurvedic culinary principles, every dish is prepared fresh on order with seasonal local produce — free from refined sugar, table salt, and preservatives.

02. Arts & Wellness

Experience hands-on pottery masterclasses, guided meditation, restorative Tibetan sound healing, and daily morning & evening yoga batches in a peaceful garden setting.

03. Soulful Community

Join an inclusive space where creativity meets calm. Immerse yourself in evening acoustic music jams, mindful reading in our library, and community retreats where time slows down.
