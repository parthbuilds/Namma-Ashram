Workshops & Circles

Immersive masterclasses, wellness circles, and soulful arts designed to slow down time, nourish the senses, and foster community connection.

Mindful Learning &
Soulful Expression

Pottery on the Wheel & Hand-Building

Master the meditative art of centering clay on the potter's wheel and sculpting timeless organic forms with master ceramic artists.

Morning Hatha Yoga & Pranayama Flow

Awaken your body and quiet the mind with daily morning asanas, conscious breathwork, and alignment practices surrounded by nature.

Tibetan Singing Bowl & Sound Healing

Experience vibrational deep relaxation through sacred singing bowls, resonant gongs, and harmonic frequencies that soothe the nervous system.

Soulful Evening Jam & Music Circle

Join acoustic community music gatherings led by soulful artists like Ravi. Bring your instrument, voice, or simply immerse in heartfelt song.

Guided Mindfulness & Meditation Circle

Cultivate inner stillness and mindful presence through guided centering sessions and breath awareness mentored by Ruby Ma'am.

Traditional Indian Folk Crafts & Artistry

Celebrate living heritage through tactile folk painting, natural block printing, sustainable fabric dyeing, and craft masterclasses.

Mindful Movement & Dance Therapy

Free your expressive spirit in light-filled hall Malang with somatic movement, ecstatic rhythm, and therapeutic dance exploration.

Ayurvedic & Macrobiotic Nutrition

Discover the curative wisdom of food with zero refined sugars and preservatives, guided by our holistic chefs and nutritionists.

Weekend Mindfulness & Digital Detox

Disconnect from screens and reconnect with your inner compass through immersive retreats with silence, grounding walks, and wholesome meals.
