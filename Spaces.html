Spaces & Banquets

Tranquil indoor halls, open-air courtyards, and verdant lawns for mindful retreats, cultural performances, workshops, and soulful celebrations.

AANGAN — Courtyard Banquet

Rustic open-air courtyard embraced by bougainvillea. Perfect for acoustic music jams, cultural performances, morning yoga, and community circles.

MALANG — Wellness & Offsite Hall

A serene indoor sanctuary with warm acoustic timber and gentle daylight. Ideal for sound baths, breathwork retreats, and leadership offsites.

Garden Lawns & Verdant Courtyards

Tree-canopied green lawns designed for barefoot grounding, sunset storytelling, open-air masterclasses, and quiet reflection under nature.

Garden Rooms & Overnight Sanctuary

Peaceful, minimalist living quarters overlooking gardens. Crafted for digital detox weekends, silent contemplation, and immersive retreats.

The Pottery & Craft Pavilion

An open-air artistic studio equipped with pottery wheels and sculpting stations for clay throwing, folk art, and mindful handicraft masterclasses.

The Mindful Library & Reading Nook

A tranquil indoor space with curated philosophy, wellness, and art literature. Ideal for peaceful co-working, journaling, and deep study.

Conscious Café & Dining Terrace

Al fresco dining celebrating Ayurvedic principles. Pure whole foods free from refined sugar and preservatives, prepared fresh to order.

Sound Bath & Meditation Sanctuary

A dedicated meditative space tuned for Tibetan singing bowl sessions, gong baths, Pranayama breathwork, and deep guided mindfulness.

Intimate Celebrations & Ceremonies

Curated outdoor & indoor settings for conscious milestone celebrations, intimate wedding rituals, Haldi circles, and family anniversaries.
