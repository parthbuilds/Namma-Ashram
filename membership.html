Detailed Events & Experiences

All materials, organic herbal refreshments, and dedicated guidance are included. Every session has capped batch sizes to maintain intimacy, stillness, and personalized attention.

Wheel Throwing & Clay Pottery

A 2-hour meditative masterclass on the potter’s wheel. Center raw terracotta clay, sculpt organic vases or bowls, and experience mindful hand-building. Includes clay, glazes & kiln firing.

Morning Hatha Yoga & Pranayama

Ground yourself in the open garden courtyards. Practice mindful traditional asanas, alignment, conscious breathwork, and deep restorative stillness. Fresh herbal infusion served post-session.

Tibetan Singing Bowl Sound Bath

Lay back on organic mats with herbal eye pillows while handcrafted Himalayan singing bowls, gentle gongs, and harmonic chimes wash over you, calming the nervous system and easing fatigue.

Booking Guidelines &
Community Etiquette

To preserve sacred peace and ensure an unhurried, mindful experience, all workshop seats are strictly limited. Advance reservation is mandatory. Please arrive 15 minutes prior to your batch start time. Comfortable, breathable attire is recommended for pottery and yoga. Clay creations require 10–14 days for drying, glazing, and kiln firing before studio pickup or courier delivery.
