Ashram Etiquette & Slow Living

Namma Ashram is a sanctuary designed to help you slow down, disconnect from city distractions, and reconnect with inner stillness. We invite all visitors to embrace slow, intentional living during their time here.

Kindly maintain the tranquil ambiance by speaking softly. We encourage a digital pause — please keep mobile devices on silent. Our outdoor garden courtyards are pet-friendly. In alignment with conscious living, tobacco and alcohol are strictly prohibited on the premises.

Frequently
asked questions

How do I book an event or workshop?
Book directly via our website’s event calendar or WhatsApp (+91 7204229002). Select your workshop or retreat, reserve your spot, and receive instant confirmation.
Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, advance booking is recommended. Spots are capped for each pottery masterclass, yoga batch, and acoustic session to preserve an intimate, serene atmosphere.
Can I walk in without a reservation?
You are always welcome to walk in for our Café, garden grounds, library, and store anytime between 8:00 AM and 9:30 PM. For workshops and overnight stays, advance reservations are strongly advised.
How long do workshops usually last?
Most pottery, yoga, and sound healing workshops run 2–3 hours. Special retreats and day festivals can extend across a full day or multi-day weekend.
Are children and pets allowed?
Yes! Many pottery, craft, and music workshops are kid-friendly. Pets are warmly welcomed across all our outdoor garden courtyard areas.
What is Namma Ashram's Café concept?
Guided by Macrobiotic and Ayurvedic principles, every meal is prepared fresh from scratch with local produce — free from refined sugar, table salt, and preservatives.
What should I bring for sessions?
Wear comfortable, breathable clothing and bring a water bottle. Quality yoga mats and meditation bolsters are provided on site.
Do you host private gatherings and corporate offsites?
Yes! Our spaces (AANGAN courtyard banquet and MALANG indoor hall) can be reserved for intimate weddings, wellness retreats, and corporate leadership offsites with custom mindful catering.
Is Namma Ashram affiliated with any religion?
No. Namma Ashram is a secular, inclusive sanctuary open to all individuals seeking peace, creativity, and mindful wellness.
