Venue Details

AANGAN is our open-air courtyard sanctuary surrounded by native trees and flowering bougainvillea. Ideal for acoustic performances, morning yoga sessions, community circles, private celebrations, intimate weddings, and art workshops.

MALANG is a serene, naturally lit indoor hall with acoustic warmth and earth-toned minimalist aesthetics. Designed specifically for sound bath healing, meditation intensives, corporate leadership offsites, dance/movement, and panel discussions.

We provide full setup support including wholesome Macrobiotic & Ayurvedic catering prepared fresh on order from our kitchen, free from refined sugar, table salt, and preservatives.

  • AANGAN Courtyard offers an open-air natural amphitheatre surrounded by lush canopy, flowering trees, and warm earthen brickwork.
  • MALANG Hall features natural daylight, wooden flooring, acoustic resonance, and meditation bolsters for immersive wellness sessions.
  • Wholesome Catering with fresh seasonal produce, artisanal herbal teas, and sattvic dining options tailored for your gathering.
