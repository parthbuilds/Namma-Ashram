4. Key Offerings & Pillars
A. The Café (Mindful & Wholesome Dining)
Philosophy: Macrobiotic & Ayurvedic culinary principles.
Purity & Integrity: Every dish cooked fresh to order using locally sourced, seasonal produce.
Clean Eating: Zero refined white sugar, zero refined table salt, zero artificial preservatives or additives.
Experience: Slow-cooked with intention (meals take 20–30 mins), outdoor lush garden seating, reading friendly, pets welcome in outdoor zones.
Signature Items: Beetroot paneer parathas, artisanal herbal teas, fresh juices, sattvic bowls, wholesome snacks, and continental mindful dishes.

5. Official FAQs (From Namma Ashram)
How do I book an event at Namma Ashram?
Book directly via our website’s event calendar or WhatsApp. Select your event, reserve your spot, and receive instant confirmation.
Do I need to book in advance?
Yes. Spots are capped for each workshop to preserve an intimate, serene atmosphere.
Can I walk in for an event?
Walk-ins are welcomed only if seats remain vacant; reservations are strongly advised.
How long do events usually last?
Workshops run 2–3 hours; retreats and day festivals can extend across a full day or multi-day weekend.
Are children allowed?
Yes, many pottery, craft, and music workshops are kid-friendly; specific adult-only sessions are noted on their respective listings.
What should I bring?
Comfortable breathable attire, water bottle, and an open heart. Yoga mats and meditation bolsters are provided on site.
What is Namma Ashram's Café concept?
Macrobiotic and Ayurvedic slow-cooked goodness made fresh from scratch without refined salt, white sugar, or preservatives.
Can I visit just to explore the space or eat?
Absolutely! Walk in anytime between 8:00 AM and 9:30 PM to enjoy the café, library, garden courtyard, and store.
Do you host private and corporate offsites?
Yes! From corporate wellness retreats and leadership offsites to intimate family gatherings and ceremonies, our spaces (AANGAN & MALANG) can be reserved with custom catering.
Is Namma Ashram affiliated with any religion?
No. Namma Ashram is a secular, inclusive sanctuary open to all individuals seeking peace, creativity, and mindful wellness.
